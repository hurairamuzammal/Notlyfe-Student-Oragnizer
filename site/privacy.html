Privacy Policy

Notlyfe is an open-source, offline-first academic workspace built for students across Android, Linux, and Windows. We collect the minimum data required to sync your workspace, encrypt your notes on-device before syncing, and never sell data or show ads.

1. Data We Process

  • Account Credentials: Email address and authentication tokens managed via Supabase Auth (or Google Sign-In when you choose it) to identify your workspace and enforce per-user Row-Level Security (RLS).
  • Academic Workspace Data: Courses, calendar events, todos, and rich-text notes you create in the app.
  • Study Files: PDFs, slides, and documents you upload to your chosen storage provider (Google Drive, WebDAV/Nextcloud, custom HTTP server, local folder, or Supabase Storage).
  • Zero Analytics & Zero Ads: Notlyfe does not embed advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics, or third-party trackers.

2. Client-Side End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)

Notes and todos are encrypted locally on your device before leaving the app using authenticated encryption (enc:v2: — AES-256-CBC + HMAC-SHA256 with separate derived encryption and MAC keys). Encryption fails closed if the local vault is not initialized, and active keys are scrubbed from memory on sign-out.

Local secrets (API keys, OAuth tokens, and vault keys) are stored in Notlyfe's encrypted vault (v3:) with the master key kept in an app-private 0600 file outside SharedPreferences. On Android, cloud backup is explicitly disabled (android:allowBackup="false") to prevent extraction of local databases and keys.

3. Google Drive Integration & Google User Data

When you connect Google Drive during setup or in Settings, Notlyfe requests only the non-sensitive, folder-scoped permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file alongside basic profile scopes (openid, email, profile).

  • Notlyfe can only create, read, update, and delete files and folders that Notlyfe itself creates inside Notlyfe/. It cannot access other files in your Google Drive.
  • OAuth tokens are encrypted locally via SecureStorageService (v3:), bound to your active Notlyfe user ID, and never shared with third parties.
  • You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from Settings → Google Drive or revoke access in your Google Account Permissions.
  • Notlyfe's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Pluggable Storage & Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) AI

  • Custom Storage Backends: If you configure WebDAV/Nextcloud, a custom HTTP server, or a local directory, your files and encrypted notes are sent directly to the endpoint you specify (HTTPS is enforced for public hosts).
  • Optional AI Assistant: AI chat is opt-in and uses your own Gemini API key stored locally in the encrypted vault. Prompts are sent directly from your device to Google's Gemini API under Google's terms.

5. Security Updates & Version Enforcement

To protect user data from vulnerabilities in outdated builds, Notlyfe checks a public minimum-version policy (version_policy.json and Supabase app_config) and sends the app version and build number in request headers (x-notlyfe-version, x-notlyfe-build). No personal or device-identifying telemetry is included in version checks.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You own your data. You can delete individual courses, notes, todos, and files directly inside Notlyfe, clear local app data on your device, or disconnect external storage at any time. To request full account deletion or ask privacy questions, contact huraira.eqeel@gmail.com or open an issue on GitHub.
